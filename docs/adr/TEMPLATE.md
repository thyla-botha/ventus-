# NNNN — Short title (one line)

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** (who made the call)

## Context

What forced the decision? What constraints / requirements were in play? Keep it specific — "we needed X by Y" beats "we wanted things to be better."

## Decision

The choice, in one or two sentences. Direct. No hedging.

## Alternatives considered

The options on the table at the time, with one-line trade-offs. This is the most important section — it's what stops future-you from re-asking "did we think about X?"

- **Option A** — [trade-off]
- **Option B** — [trade-off]
- **Option C (chosen)** — [trade-off]

## Consequences

What we accept by choosing this. Both the good (what it unlocks) and the bad (the risks / debt we now carry). If there are conditions under which we'd revisit, say so.

## Notes

Anything else useful — links to commits, tickets, prior incidents.
